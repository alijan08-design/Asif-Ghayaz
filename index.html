About Asif Ghayaz

Asif Ghayaz is a Pakistani artist known for finely rendered pen-and-ink drawings and mixed-media paintings. Trained under the mentorship of Eqbal Mehdi, Asif’s practice blends disciplined draftsmanship with a contemplative use of space and texture. His works—ranging from intimate portraits to architectural studies—explore memory, place, and quiet narratives through meticulous linework.

Beyond gallery exhibitions across Pakistan, Asif’s illustrations and artworks have been commissioned for institutional publications and calendars, demonstrating a practice that moves comfortably between illustration and fine art.

Frequently Asked Questions

Buying, pricing, the calligraphy and caring for original art.

Who is Asif Ghayaz?

Asif Ghayaz is a Pakistani artist known for finely rendered pen-and-ink drawings, landscape paintings and Islamic calligraphy. He trained under Eqbal Mehdi, has exhibited across Pakistan, and his work has been used in institutional publications.

What kind of artwork can I see on this website?

The galleries cover pen-and-ink masterpieces and portraits, horse studies, architectural drawings, landscape paintings, Islamic calligraphy, still life and abstract work. Every piece shown is an original artwork by Asif Ghayaz.

How can I buy an original artwork or commission a piece?

Send a message through the Contact form, by email or on WhatsApp and mention the artwork you like. For a commission, describe the subject, size and style you have in mind and you will receive a reply with the next steps.

How much does an artwork cost?

Each piece is original and is priced individually, depending on its size, medium and complexity. Prices are shared on request: contact the studio with the artwork you are interested in and you will be sent its price.

What does the calligraphy say, and what does it mean?

The calligraphy pieces are original compositions in Arabic script, painted in colour. If you would like the text, translation and meaning of a particular piece, send its number (for example “Islamic Calligraphy No. 7”) through the Contact form or WhatsApp.

What is pen-and-ink art?

Pen-and-ink art is made by building an image with fine lines, dots and cross-hatching in ink on paper. Because ink cannot be erased, every mark is deliberate. That patient, detailed technique is what gives Asif Ghayaz's drawings their texture and depth.

How do I decorate a wall with artwork?

Hang a single large piece at eye level, with its centre about 145–150 cm from the floor. For several smaller works, group them with even gaps of 5–8 cm so they read as one arrangement. Match the frame size to the wall: a piece should fill roughly two-thirds of the width of the furniture beneath it. Use soft, indirect light and keep original ink and paper works out of direct sunlight.

Can I visit the studio or see the work in person?

Work has been exhibited in galleries across Pakistan, and studio visits are available by appointment. Use the Contact section to arrange a time.
